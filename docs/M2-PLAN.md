## Decisions (approved 2026-11-08)
1. **Battery position.** The model has a flat underbody panel at y 0.095–0.109 and the cabin floor 25–80 mm above it. Recommend a flat tray in that gap, resting on the underbody: x ±0.45, z −0.10…0.55, 20 mm deep at the back and stepping up to 42 mm at the front where the floor rises. It touches nothing, is hidden when assembled, and slides out sideways in stage 5. The HV cable leaves at its rear centre into the space behind the seats. A uniform 70 mm pack would cut into the seats and the cabin floor.
2. **Model shells our parts must cross.** The underbody, wheelhouse liners (x ±0.60) and bay side walls (x ±0.50) are one connected shell in the model, so they cannot be hidden. Driveshafts, rear links and coilovers have to pass through those walls, as they pass through the inner wheel arches on the real car. Recommend treating those crossings as expected contacts. The side pods are lined with inner duct panels; recommend moving the panels directly behind each side-intake grille into the hidden guide group so the radiators can sit there, then checking from outside that no hole shows.
3. **Clash check precision.** Recommend testing real triangles (part mesh vs model mesh) instead of bounding boxes, plus a per-part list of expected contacts (disc ↔ caliper, upright ↔ caliper, tips ↔ exhaust openings). Bounding boxes flag round parts falsely: a disc's square box hits the rim barrel the disc itself clears.
4. **Explode length.** The outside-in teardown needs about 5.2 s instead of ~4 s (stage 4 runs 2 s; stage 5 starts at 4.2 s).
5. **Dev keys.** K = segment colours, S = solo the selected group, B = original engine-bay ghost.
